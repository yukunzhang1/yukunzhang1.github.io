Research series · Seven papers研究系列 · 七篇论文

Generative AI Economics

Capital, Markets, and Information资本、市场与信息

Seven papers examine how generative AI changes the economics of intelligence production, reorganizes markets and platforms, and reshapes information and incentives. The series brings together complementary theoretical and computational perspectives on value creation, distribution, and governance.七篇论文考察生成式 AI 如何改变智能生产的经济机制、重组市场与平台，并影响信息与激励。该系列结合相互补充的理论和计算研究，讨论价值创造、分配与治理。

About the series关于系列

The papers share questions about costs, incentives, and welfare. Each develops its own model or computational setting; the thematic order is a reading guide rather than a single sequence of formal deductions.各篇共享有关成本、激励和福利的问题，但分别采用独立的模型或计算设定。主题顺序用于引导阅读，并不表示七篇构成一条统一的形式推导链。
